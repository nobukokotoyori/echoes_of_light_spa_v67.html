"Those who give first become the light of hope and create the world."
Share your action of "Tokuzumi" as part of the 10,000 stories of hope.

Love (愛)

Show gratitude to family or friends and bring a smile to their faces. Caring for those close to you is the first step to healing the world.

Connection (繋)

Spread this circle of hope via SNS or by inviting friends. Your voice becomes the bridge that connects hearts.

Creativity (創)

Help with event setup or enliven the venue through song or dance. Experience the joy of sharing your talents.

Purity (清)

Pick up trash and polish the mirror that is our Earth. Purifying the venue is an act of respect for your own heart and the planet.

Prayer (祈)

Offer a minute of silence and write down your "Life Mission." Find peace in the silence that restores dignity.

Wisdom (学)

Explore Japanese traditional culture and history at the KIZUNA Exhibition. A compass for opening the future.

Pay It Forward (送)

Donate for the next person's meal. A form of economy where abundance continues to circulate across borders.
